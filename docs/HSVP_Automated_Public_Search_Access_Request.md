**Subject:** Request for written permission and approved conditions for controlled read-only Plot Status Enquiry integration

Respected Sir/Madam,

Bharat Properties is developing an internal property verification workflow for its real-estate inventory. We request HSVP's written guidance and, if permissible, explicit authorization to use the public Plot Status Enquiry facility for controlled, read-only property verification from our internal CRM.

We understand that the HSVP website Terms of Use restrict commercial reproduction/copying without prior written approval and reserve the right to restrict access for disruptive crawlers, spiders, or scrapers. We will not enable automated collection or copy/store portal data unless HSVP provides written permission and the approved conditions are recorded.

## Requested scope

1. Read-only lookup using the public Plot Status Enquiry fields: Urban Estate, Sector, and Plot Number.
2. Use only property-level fields that HSVP expressly approves for this purpose, such as property identifier, estate, sector, property category/type, area, status, possession and other non-personal attributes, where available.
3. No login, OTP, CAPTCHA, authentication, access-control or rate-limit bypass.
4. No proxy/IP rotation, parallel load generation, or attempts to evade technical restrictions.
5. No collection or storage of owner/allottee names, personal contact details, Aadhaar/PAN, private addresses, or other personal information as part of the proposed first phase.
6. Results will remain provisional reference information and will not be represented as legal title verification. Official HSVP records will be treated as authoritative.

## Conditions requested from HSVP

Please confirm in writing:

- Whether automated read-only lookups of the public Plot Status Enquiry page are permitted for internal commercial CRM verification.
- Whether a sanctioned API, data export, scheduled file, or other preferred integration method is available.
- The exact permitted data fields and whether storage/caching within a private internal CRM is allowed.
- The maximum requests per minute/day, minimum interval, concurrency limit, operating hours, and any other rate/concurrency requirements.
- The permitted retention period, refresh frequency, source attribution/disclaimer requirements, and deletion obligations.
- The required technical contact or process for reporting errors, access challenges, or changes to the portal.
- The approval's effective date, expiry/review date, and the reference number to cite in our system.

Until we receive written authorization and the above limits, our integration will remain disabled and we will not run automated searches or bulk collection.

We would appreciate your guidance on the appropriate procedure and any prescribed application form or agreement.
